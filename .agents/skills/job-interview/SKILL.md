# Job Interview Preparation

Use this skill when preparing for an upcoming client or employer interview.

## Instructions
1. Follow the canonical procedure in [.claude/commands/interview.md](../../.claude/commands/interview.md) and [.claude/skills/job-application-assistant/07-interview-prep.md](../../.claude/skills/job-application-assistant/07-interview-prep.md).
2. Review company research and job requirements.
3. Formulate STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result) responses anchored strictly in verified experience.
4. Prepare strategic questions to ask the client/interviewer about technical architecture, expectations, and contract milestones.
