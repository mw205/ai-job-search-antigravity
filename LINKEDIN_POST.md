---

Most developers and freelancers treat the job search as a spray-and-pray game.

The result? Low response rates, generic cover letters that sound like ChatGPT, and hours wasted formatting LaTeX and checking ATS margins.

A few months ago, Mads Lorentzen showed how an AI engineer can approach this like a software system by open-sourcing `ai-job-search` for Claude Code.

Today, I’m open-sourcing the **Google Antigravity Edition**:

🚀 **AI Job Search for Google Antigravity**
A local, multi-agent career operating system that turns Google Antigravity into your full-time job discovery and application partner.

Here’s how it works on your local machine:

1️⃣ **Automated Multi-Portal Discovery**
Built-in CLI scrapers search LinkedIn, FreeHire, and developer boards for remote, freelance, and contract positions matching your exact stack.

2️⃣ **Prompt-Injection Defense**
Job postings are untrusted web data. An automated safety gate filters hidden HTML, tracking tokens, and malicious prompts before the AI ever reads the text.

3️⃣ **Two-Agent Drafter-Reviewer Architecture**
The primary Antigravity agent drafts your application, and an adversarial "Hiring Manager" subagent independently critiques it against real company research and your candidate truth base.

4️⃣ **Strict LaTeX Compilation & ATS Parseability Audit**
No broken formatting or guesswork:
• Your CV compiles to **exactly 2 pages** with `lualatex`.
• Your cover letter compiles to **exactly 1 page** with `xelatex`.
• A mechanical Python verifier audits the text layer to ensure Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) read every contact detail and keyword cleanly.

5️⃣ **100% Privacy-Preserving**
Runs completely on your own machine. Your contact details, salary targets, and application history stay in your private repository.
